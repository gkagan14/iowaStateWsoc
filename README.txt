BIG 12 WOMEN'S SOCCER — 2026 SCOUTING DASHBOARD
================================================
Prepared 23 Nov 2026 · Iowa State staff pack

WHAT IS IN HERE
---------------
index.html                                 The dashboard. Open it in any browser. No internet,
                                           server or install needed — all data is embedded.
big12-soccer-team-stats-2025-2026.csv      Every team, both seasons (2025 + 2026), all games and
                                           Big 12 only, 33 columns.
big12-soccer-game-logs-2025-2026.csv       Every match for both seasons with box-score lines
                                           (shots, SOG, corners, saves, fouls) and goal timelines.
IowaState_vs_KansasState_match_prep.pdf    Worked example of the printable prep sheet.

HOW TO USE THE DASHBOARD
------------------------
· Tabs: Conference board · Team pages · Matchup lab · Game explorer · 2025 → 2026 · 2027
  projection · Method & sources.
· Header: switch season (2026 / 2025), switch scope (All games / Big 12 play), set "My team".
  "My team" and the 2027 roster-turnover marks are saved in the browser on that device.
· Prep sheet (PDF): in the Matchup lab or on any opponent's team page. It opens a print view —
  use your browser's Print → Save as PDF (Letter) to file it or email it.
· Export CSV on the conference board exports whichever season and scope you are viewing.

PUBLISHING IT
-------------
This is a single self-contained HTML file, so "publishing" just means putting index.html
somewhere people can open it:
· Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) — drag this whole folder onto the page, get a URL.
· GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages / Vercel — commit index.html to a repo and enable Pages.
· SharePoint, Google Drive or OneDrive folder — staff download and open it locally.
· Tablet: open it in Safari/Chrome, then "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like icon.
Keep the CSVs next to it if staff want the raw tables.

WHAT THE DATA IS
----------------
2026 season: 222 matches, 221 with full box scores. 2025 season: 236 matches, 215 with full
box scores. Both reconcile to the Big 12 Conference official statistics on wins, losses, ties,
goals for and goals against. Known gap: BYU and UCF 2025 out-of-conference stat lines are
scores-only (their 2025 schedule pages are not archived).
Provenance and method notes are inside the dashboard under "Method & sources".
